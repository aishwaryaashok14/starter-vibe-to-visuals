# Visual direction reference

Use these as reasoning patterns. Adapt them to the product and buying moment instead of assigning them mechanically.

## Quiet editorial

- Communicates: calm, care, considered value.
- Palette: warm neutral field plus one cool and one warm accent.
- Type: expressive serif headline with a plain sans-serif for facts and prices.
- Light: soft directional daylight with visible shadow edges.
- Composition: generous space, one dominant object, controlled crops.
- Materials: ceramic, paper, brushed metal, woven or matte surfaces.
- Risk: can become generic beige minimalism. Preserve contrast and one memorable colour.

## Playful object theatre

- Communicates: energy, curiosity, approachable design.
- Palette: cream field with two saturated, contrasting accents.
- Type: rounded or geometric sans-serif with large scale changes.
- Light: clean studio light with graphic shadows.
- Composition: stacked objects, unexpected scale, simple colour planes.
- Materials: glossy ceramic, coloured metal, translucent acrylic, coated paper.
- Risk: can look childish. Keep the product photography controlled and the copy concise.

## Warm handmade

- Communicates: tactility, small-batch care, everyday warmth.
- Palette: clay, oat, moss, ink, one sun-warmed accent.
- Type: humanist serif or softened grotesk; avoid fake handwritten fonts.
- Light: window light with natural falloff and surface texture.
- Composition: close crops, hands only when useful, visible material imperfections.
- Materials: clay, linen, wood, recycled paper, uncoated finishes.
- Risk: can drift into rustic clichés. Avoid dried flowers, twine, and staged craft props unless central to the product.

## Bold graphic

- Communicates: confidence, clarity, visual impact.
- Palette: two strong colours, one pale field, black or deep ink.
- Type: heavy grotesk or condensed display face paired with a neutral text face.
- Light: hard light or flash with deliberate shadow geometry.
- Composition: frontal views, tight crops, large typography, simple grids.
- Materials: lacquer, glass, metal, glossy print.
- Risk: typography can overpower the product. Keep one clear focal object.

## Soft utility

- Communicates: usefulness, order, quiet reliability.
- Palette: stone, blue-grey, soft green, one functional signal colour.
- Type: neutral sans-serif with strong information hierarchy.
- Light: even natural light; minimal drama.
- Composition: clear scale, repeatable angles, visible function.
- Materials: powder-coated metal, recycled plastic, rubber, canvas.
- Risk: can feel like a catalog without personality. Add one distinctive crop or colour rule.

## Dark cinematic

- Communicates: intimacy, focus, after-dark ritual.
- Palette: ink, aubergine or forest, metallic highlight, one luminous accent.
- Type: restrained high-contrast serif or condensed sans-serif.
- Light: narrow pools of light, reflective edges, deep but readable shadows.
- Composition: close details, foreground occlusion, slow reveal.
- Materials: smoked glass, polished metal, dark wood, glazed ceramic.
- Risk: product details and prices can lose legibility. Keep ecommerce surfaces lighter than campaign imagery.

## Route construction rule

The three proposed routes must differ in their underlying logic, not only their colour palette. Each route should change at least four of: emotional signal, typography, light, composition, material emphasis, pace, and copy style.
