02

Create and lock the store

Three useful decisions give the skill enough information to recommend a coherent visual direction.

11 MIN

Answer the three questions with specific, visible details.

Name visible objects with different shapes or scales.
Name a person, situation, and desired small change.
Add a commercial position, one quality to preserve, and one thing to avoid. Ask for a recommendation if unsure.
Useful input“Small travel organisers for people packing for frequent weekend trips.”
Too broad“A cool luxury lifestyle brand for everyone.”
Use $vibe-to-store in workshop mode. My product set: [complete the field above] My customer moment: [complete the field above] My taste boundary: [complete the field above] Propose three clearly different visual directions. Explain the palette, typography, lighting, composition, materials, one risk, and why each direction fits. Then wait for me to choose.

Choose one direction, or combine two named qualities once. Then create the Store Kit.

Quiet Focus

Oat, moss, cobalt. Editorial type, diffused daylight, generous space.

Playful Reset

Cream, tomato, cornflower. Geometric type, graphic shadows, stacked objects.

Night Study

Ink, lilac, silver. Condensed type, pools of light, slow close-ups.

Develop direction [name]. Lock the store and create one file named STORE-KIT.md. It must contain exactly three complete products, five palette colours, visual rules, approved website and campaign copy, non-negotiables, and an avoid list. Validate it against the Store Kit schema before you finish.
Ready stateOne file with three complete products, five colours, visual rules, and approved copy.
If you are stuckUse fallback/STORE-KIT.md. Do not improvise missing products, prices, or claims during the workshop.
03

Generate the product reference

One approved family image gives the website, social image, and video the same product identity.

10 MIN

Generate all three products together before creating campaign imagery.

Read STORE-KIT.md. Create one high-resolution landscape product-family image containing exactly the three specified products, clearly separated into left, centre, and right regions so each product can be cropped for a website card. Preserve each silhouette, material, and colour. Use the chosen lighting, surface, and composition rules. No text, logo, packaging invention, extra products, people, or unsupported claims. Completion check: all three products are distinct, match STORE-KIT.md, and can each be isolated with a crop.
Ready stateThree distinct products with the correct material, colour, and silhouette.
If you are stuckReduce the scene to a plain surface and one light source. If one object is wrong, regenerate the family rather than carrying the mismatch forward.
04

Build the store

The website turns the visual direction into a complete buying path.

20 MIN

Ask Sites to build the store from STORE-KIT.md and product-family.png.

Read STORE-KIT.md and use product-family.png as the visual reference. Create a responsive one-page ecommerce Site with the exact store promise, three products, prices, product detail view, add-to-cart action, visible cart count, and total. Apply each product's crop hint and zoom. Preserve the approved visual direction and factual claims. Do not add products, pages, testimonials, discounts, checkout, accounts, or new claims. Test the cart and mobile layout, then publish the Site privately.
Ready stateA published Site with three products, working product view, correct cart, and usable mobile layout.
Using the Site with Pomelli?

Pomelli needs a public URL. Change the Site's audience only if public sharing is allowed. Otherwise keep it private and use another social-image route.

If you are stuckIf Sites is unavailable after two minutes, open website-starter/index.html and run the same prompt without the final publishing instruction. A simulated cart is enough; do not connect payment processing.
05

Create one social image

A campaign image should lead with one message while preserving the product reference.

12 MIN

Attach the product reference and use the prompt below to make one 1080 × 1350 image.

Attach product-family.png and read STORE-KIT.md. Create one 1080 × 1350 social campaign image with one dominant product and the approved social line. Preserve the product's exact shape, material, and colour. Apply the chosen palette, lighting, composition, and type direction. You may change crop, scale, setting, and shadow. Do not redesign the product, add packaging, add products, or invent claims. Completion check: the product matches the reference and one message leads the frame.

06

Build the three-beat video

The hook, product, and payoff give a short sequence visible progression.

15 MIN

1 · Hook

0–2.5 seconds. One detail and a reason to continue.

2 · Product

2.5–5.2 seconds. Exact product name and price.

3 · Payoff

5.2–8 seconds. The collection and store promise.

Use the prompt below to create the same three beats in your available video tool.

Read STORE-KIT.md. Use product-family.png and the chosen social image. Create a silent 1080 × 1920 sequence lasting 6–8 seconds with exactly three scenes: hook, product, payoff. Use the approved video lines and exact product name and price. Preserve product shape, material, colour, and the chosen motion direction. Do not add new products, claims, spinning objects, or extra scenes. Completion check: a viewer can understand the store and product when the video plays muted.
Choose a video tool

Google Flow

Upload product-family.png and the social image as references, then use the prompt above. Generate one short clip. Switch routes after two minutes if generation is unavailable.

Editor or browser loop

Arrange one still per beat in CapCut or Adobe Express, or replace the images and lines in video-template/index.html.

Ready stateThree different beats, readable text, recognisable products, and a clear ending.
If you are stuckSubmit the working browser loop or a three-frame animatic. Audio and complex transitions are stretch work.
