# Dusk Desk

## Store promise

Small desk objects for a calmer late study session.

## Customer and moment

Students resetting a crowded desk before working late and wanting the space to feel focused without becoming clinical.

## Commercial position

Considered but attainable.

## Chosen direction

Night Study. Deep ink and forest tones create an after-dark setting, while silver details and a warm signal colour keep the products legible.

## Visual rules

- Palette: Night Ink `#151820`, Forest `#29483B`, Lilac `#B9A9CF`, Silver `#C4C7CA`, Ember `#D36A3A`.
- Typography: condensed sans-serif display type, neutral sans-serif body, and monospaced prices.
- Lighting: narrow pools of warm artificial light with readable reflective edges.
- Composition: close details, shallow depth, clear silhouettes, and controlled negative space.
- Materials and props: dark wood, powder-coated metal, ceramic, aluminium, and one sheet of plain paper.
- Motion: slow reveals and direct cuts. No spinning or floating products.

## Voice and campaign copy

- Voice: focused, calm, and direct.
- Hero headline: Make room for the next hour.
- Supporting line: Three desk objects for a quieter late session.
- Social line: Reset the desk. Keep the focus.
- Video beat 1: Clear the noise.
- Video beat 2: Forest Focus Timer · ₹1,650.
- Video beat 3: Make room for the next hour.

## Non-negotiables

- Preserve the exact product names, descriptions, prices, shapes, materials, colours, and approved claims.
- Use `assets/product-family.png` as the visual identity reference.
- Keep every product usable and clearly scaled as a desk object.

## Avoid

- Neon gaming imagery.
- Productivity or concentration claims.
- Floating products or impossible reflections.
- Busy desks and visible brand-name devices.
- New products, claims, prices, or materials.
