# Soft Hours

## Store promise

Small objects for a slower evening.

## Customer and moment

Apartment dwellers marking the end of the workday with a simple evening ritual.

## Commercial position

Considered but attainable.

## Chosen direction

Quiet editorial with one playful colour accent. Restrained layouts and tactile ceramics feel considered, while cobalt and tomato red prevent generic beige minimalism.

## Visual rules

- Palette: Warm Cream `#F5EDDD`, Oat `#D8C7A8`, Cobalt `#315E82`, Sage `#9AAF92`, Tomato `#C9473D`.
- Typography: expressive editorial serif headlines, neutral sans-serif body, and compact monospaced prices.
- Lighting: late-afternoon side light with long, soft-edged shadows.
- Composition: one dominant object, simple blocks, tactile crops, and generous space.
- Materials and props: glazed and unglazed ceramic, pale stone, one incense stick, and plain paper.
- Motion: slow push-ins, simple cuts, and shadow movement. Products never spin.

## Non-negotiables

- Preserve the exact product names, descriptions, prices, shapes, materials, colours, and approved claims.
- Use `product-family.png` as the visual identity reference.
- Keep the ecommerce experience to one collection and a simulated cart.

## Avoid

- Spa imagery and wellness clichés.
- Dried flowers, towels, bath settings, and cosmetic packaging.
- All-beige minimalism.
- Hard-sell discounts or invented scarcity.
- New products, claims, prices, or materials.
